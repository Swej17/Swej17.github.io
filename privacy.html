Privacy Policy

GTA Clipper Uploader · Effective November 18, 2026

Who uses this app

GTA Clipper Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Swej Gaming YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses one Google permission: youtube.upload. It uses it only to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to schedule when they go public. It does not read your email, contacts, files or other Google data.

What it stores

It stores the sign-in token Google issues on the owner's computer only, so that approved uploads can run. It also keeps a local record of each upload: the video ID, link, time and status. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and YouTube's own APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app has no analytics or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Data obtained from the YouTube API is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as YouTube's developer policies require. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that, deleting the app's local folder removes the stored token.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
